How
Pick a joint, ENABLE it, jog it where you want, then press RECORD WAYPOINT. Repeat for each step. A joint that is disabled when you record is stored as “–”, which means leave it alone on playback — this arm has no position feedback, so a disabled joint has nothing to report.

About this tab
The selected joint’s card shows its full engineering controls here: the soft‑stop envelope, LOCK THIS AXIS, its speed setting and its pin assignment. Those are hidden on Teach so they do not compete with RECORD WAYPOINT — not disabled, just out of the way.

On the bridge route joint-limits.csv is read automatically on every connect, because opening the port resets the board and the firmware keeps nothing across a reset. Use the picker on the file:// route, or to try a file that is not the one on disk.

Why some rows cannot be played
A pose is READY only when its recorded entry path can be read back step by step. Rows written before the console could record a path carry free text instead, so there is nothing to drive — they stay REFERENCE ONLY until the move is driven and recorded again.

PLAY on a row stays dead until that same row has been loaded. It is not a second way to drive the arm: loading puts the recorded steps in front of you first, and PLAY then runs the same guarded playback the Teach tab uses. The straight line between two safe poses can still drive the claw through the bench.
